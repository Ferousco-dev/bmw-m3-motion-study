# Review Rules

## Severity

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CRITICAL | Breaks first use, or makes the product unusable/unreadable for a real visitor |
| HIGH | Materially damages the experience; visible to any careful viewer |
| MEDIUM | Noticeable to a designer; erodes the premium read |
| LOW | Polish; only visible on close inspection |

## Finding format

    Problem
    Why it happens
    Severity
    Recommended solution
    Expected visual impact
    Expected technical impact

## Scoring (Final Judge)

Visual Design, Typography, Motion, UX, Art Direction, Performance,
Accessibility, Responsive Design, Premium Feel, Overall, each /10.

The Final Judge must always state:
1. What is working. 2. What feels cheap. 3. What feels unfinished.
4. What is distracting. 5. What to fix. 6. **What must not be changed.**

## Definition of finished

No CRITICAL or HIGH findings open; every score ≥ 8; a first-time visitor on a
cold load reaches a usable experience in under two seconds; and the page reads
as one system rather than a set of separate effects.
